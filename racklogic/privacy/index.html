RackLogic Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

RackLogic is a desk for homelab parts. This policy explains what the website at desk.getsigncheck.com and the RackLogic Android app do with information. The developer is Jerome Anderson. RackLogic has no accounts and no ads.

1. Information stored on your device

The desk keeps your own setup in the browser on that phone or computer. That setup can include:

  • A US ZIP code you type, and how many miles you are willing to drive
  • Watches you add: a part name or a category, the words you choose, and the most you will pay
  • Listings you pin: the title, the link, and the price
  • Parts you mute
  • When you last opened the desk, so it can show what changed since then

That setup stays in the browser. Clearing the site data for desk.getsigncheck.com, or uninstalling the Android app, removes it from the device.

2. What is sent so the page can be drawn

When the desk refreshes, the browser sends that setup to the RackLogic server so the page can show listings that match your watches and ZIP. The server uses it for that response. It does not create an account, and it does not keep a copy of your watches, pins, mute list, or visit times.

If you type a ZIP, the server asks Zippopotam which place that ZIP refers to, then stores the place under that ZIP so distance can be shown. The stored record is the ZIP and its place. It is not stored under your name, because RackLogic does not have your name.

3. The shared listing feed

One server collects public for-sale posts from eBay, Reddit's r/homelabsales, and ServeTheHome. A post can include a title, a price, a link, and a location the seller wrote. That feed is the same for everyone who opens the desk. It is not a profile of you.

Opening a listing leaves RackLogic and opens eBay, Reddit, or ServeTheHome. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

4. Information RackLogic does not collect

The app and the desk do not ask for, and do not store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or a password
  • Your precise location from the phone. A ZIP is used only if you type one
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • A Google Play identity. If you install from Google Play, that account belongs to Google
  • Payment details. RackLogic has no in-app purchase

RackLogic does not use an advertising or analytics package, and it does not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

5. Connection data

The public desk is delivered through Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes the connection, including the IP address, to route the request. RackLogic's own application log does not save that address as a profile.

6. How information is used

  • To show listings that match the watches and ZIP on that device
  • To show about how far a listing is from a ZIP you typed
  • To collect the shared feed of public parts listings
  • To answer an email you send about the app

7. How long information is kept

Your watches and ZIP stay on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. The shared listing feed is kept so the desk can show current and recent posts. A ZIP-to-place lookup is kept so the same ZIP does not have to be looked up again. Email you send is kept long enough to answer it and to keep a record of the request.

8. Your choices

You can leave the ZIP blank, delete a watch, and clear the site data for desk.getsigncheck.com. Because the server does not keep your desk, clearing the device removes the copy RackLogic has of it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask what personal information is held, to correct it, or to ask for it to be deleted. Email the address below. For the information in section 1, the copy is on your device.

9. Children

RackLogic is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

The desk is served over HTTPS. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure.

11. International use

The desk is operated from the United States. If you use it from another country, the information described above is processed in the United States.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will change. The current version is always at https://getsigncheck.com/racklogic/privacy/.
